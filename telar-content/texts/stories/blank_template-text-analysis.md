**

One key difference between the Spanish and Nahuatl translations lies in the way the festival in honor of Tlaloc was described. For example, the Spanish translation wrote a great deal about the violent consequences for those who break the rules of the fast, writing immense details about how cruel they could be and really painting a picture of how "torturous" the punishments were. They spent only a few short lines talking about why this festival was important and the joy and gratitude for life behind it.

  

By comparison, the Nahuatl translation places greater emphasis on the ritual practices themselves including communal dancing with maize and presenting your crops to others, which highlights the cultural and spiritual significance of these activities.

  

This shows with the Spaniards' prioritization lies when creating this document: they want to detail the “horrors” of sacrifice and the other indigenous religious practices in order to better defend their forced Christianization and wiping away native cultures/religions. Emphasizing punishment and discipline serves colonial narratives that framed native traditions as morally wrongand therefore "in need" of Christian reform.
